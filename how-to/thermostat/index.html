Air conditioning and thermostat

Stay cool and keep energy use low.

  1. The thermostat is at location. Tap the screen to wake it.
  2. Choose Cool and set your temperature. Most guests like 74 to 76 degrees.
  3. Keep windows and doors closed while the air conditioning runs.
  4. When you head out for the day, set it to 78 degrees or higher.
  5. Before checkout, set it to setting.
If the house does not cool, check that the mode says Cool and the fan says Auto. Then message us. A/C issues are our top priority.
