Trash and recycling

Keep the home tidy and the neighbors happy.

  1. Bins are at location. Place bags in the trash bin and clean cans and bottles in the recycling bin.
  2. Pickup days are days. If your stay overlaps a pickup, roll the bins to the curb the night before.
  3. Bring empty bins back to location after pickup.
  4. Please flush only toilet paper. Wipes and feminine products belong in the trash.
Wildlife is active here. Keep lids closed and no food scraps outside.
