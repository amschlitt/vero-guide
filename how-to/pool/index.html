How to · 1381 3rd Court

Private heated pool

Safe, easy pool days.

  1. Latch the backyard gate behind you every time. The fence keeps little ones and pets safe.
  2. The pool is heated season and temperature range. Please do not change the heater settings.
  3. Children and non-swimmers stay within arm's reach of an adult at all times. There is no lifeguard.
  4. No diving. The pool is depth deep. No glass in or near the pool.
  5. Leave the pool at the first sound of thunder. Afternoon storms are common.
  6. Rinse off after sunscreen to help keep the water clear.
Pool service visits on day. The crew needs backyard access, so please keep the yard clear that morning.
Pets stay out of the pool and should not drink pool water.
