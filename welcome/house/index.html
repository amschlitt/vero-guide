Around the house

  1. Air conditioning: the thermostat is at location. Keep windows and doors closed while it runs, and set it to 78 degrees or higher when you are out.
  2. Kitchen: cook as much or as little as you like. The dishwasher is yours to use.
  3. Laundry: the washer and dryer are at location. Clean the lint trap after each load.
  4. Bathroom: please flush only toilet paper.
  5. Trash and recycling: bins are at location. Pickup is days. If your stay overlaps, roll bins to the curb the night before.
  6. Breaker panel and water shut-off: locations. Please call us before touching either unless it is an emergency.

Pool and fire pit safety

  1. The pool sits in a fenced backyard. There is no lifeguard, so please keep an eye on swimmers.
  2. Children and non-swimmers stay within arm's reach of an adult at all times.
  3. No diving. The pool is depth deep. Do not swim alone or after drinking.
  4. No glass in or near the pool.
  5. Use the fire pit only with an adult present, never leave it burning unattended, and put it out fully before bed.
  6. Pets stay out of the pool and should not drink the pool water.
  7. Leave the pool at the first sound of thunder. Afternoon storms are common.
  8. The pool is heated season and temperature range. Pool service visits on day.

House rules

  1. Guests: up to 4 registered guests. No parties, events or additional overnight guests.
  2. The renter must be 21 or older, and the primary guest must be present at check-in.
  3. Quiet hours are 10 pm to 8 am. Our neighbors live here year-round.
  4. No smoking of any kind indoors. Extra cleaning fees may apply based on evidence of smoking.
  5. Pets are welcome with advance approval: up to 2 small dogs or one medium dog, with a $150 pet fee per 14 days. Please pick up after your pets and never leave them unattended outside.
  6. Cameras: a Ring doorbell camera at the front door and an exterior camera on the shed facing the pool equipment. There are no cameras inside.
  7. Please tell us right away if something breaks. Guests are responsible for damage during their stay.
  8. A safe with key is provided. We are not responsible for its contents.
  9. We may enter with reasonable notice.
  10. Your reservation ends at the checkout date and time, which serves as notice to vacate. A late checkout fee applies.
  11. The primary guest is responsible for all other guests and for following all local and state laws.

Emergencies

  • Emergency: call 911. Address for responders: 1381 3rd Court, Vero Beach, FL.
  • Nearest hospital: Cleveland Clinic Indian River Hospital, about 10 minutes north up Indian River Blvd. 1000 36th St, Vero Beach, FL.
  • A/C, water, power or lockout: message us in the Airbnb or VRBO app first, any hour.
  • Hurricane season (June through November): if a storm threatens, follow Atlantic updates from the National Hurricane Center at nhc.noaa.gov and any evacuation orders from Indian River County.
