Things to do

Beaches, errands and attractions nearby.

Beaches

All are public with free parking. Rip currents are common on this coast. If caught in one, stay calm, call for help and swim parallel to shore.

South Beach Park. Our closest beach: quieter, with a boardwalk through the dunes. Check lifeguard hours. 1704 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Humiston Park. Easiest beach day: lifeguards, restrooms, and Ocean Drive shops and restaurants steps away. Busiest, so arrive early. 3000 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Jaycee Beach Park. Family favorite with a long boardwalk, picnic pavilions and a playground. Check lifeguard hours. 4200 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Round Island Oceanside Park. Ocean beach plus manatee viewing and kayaking on the river side. Check lifeguard hours. 2201 S Hwy A1A, Vero Beach, FL

Wabasso Beach Park. Surfing and snorkeling on a near-shore reef. Small lot, so go off-hours. Wabasso Beach Park, CR 510 at A1A, Vero Beach, FL

Treasure Shores Park. Dunes, picnic area, pirate-ship playground and uncrowded sand. No lifeguard. 11300 N Hwy A1A, Vero Beach, FL

Within a mile: groceries and errands

Publix. The closest full supermarket, with a pharmacy and liquor store. 1255 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Walmart Neighborhood Market. Groceries, household basics and a pharmacy. 1750 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Walgreens. Pharmacy. 915 S US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

CVS. Pharmacy. 1706 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Wawa. Gas, snacks and quick sandwiches. 1180 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Starbucks. Coffee on the go. 1235 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Einstein Bros. Bagels. Bagels and coffee for an easy morning. 1226 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Things to do

Ocean Drive shopping. More than 20 local boutiques and galleries near Humiston Park. Ocean Dr and Beachland Blvd, Vero Beach, FL

Vero Beach Farmers Market. Saturdays, 8 am to noon, at Humiston Park. 3000 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

McKee Botanical Garden. A beautiful local treasure with 18 acres of tropical gardens. 350 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Pelican Island National Wildlife Refuge. Trails and birding at the nation's first wildlife refuge. 4055 Wildlife Way, Vero Beach, FL

Environmental Learning Center. Pontoon and paddling tours of the Indian River Lagoon. A great day with school-age kids, and easy to combine with a trip to Sebastian Inlet. 255 Live Oak Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Sebastian Inlet State Park. Fishing, surfing and the McLarty Treasure Museum. 9700 S Hwy A1A, Melbourne Beach, FL

Vero Beach Museum of Art. A great rainy-day stop. 3001 Riverside Park Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Riverside Theatre. Live shows year-round. 3250 Riverside Park Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Indian River Citrus Museum. Local citrus history in a 1935 landmark. 2140 14th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Hallstrom House. A preserved 1918 pioneer homestead. 1723 Old Dixie Hwy SW, Vero Beach, FL

Historical Society Railroad Station. The restored 1903 depot. 2336 14th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Historic Dodgertown. The Dodgers' spring-training home from 1948. 3901 26th St, Vero Beach, FL

Summer Crush Vineyard & Winery. A great day out for live music and drinks, about 30 minutes away. Check their website to see who is playing. 4200 Johnston Rd, Fort Pierce, FL
