Things to do

Beaches and attractions nearby.

Beaches

All are public with free parking. Rip currents are common on this coast. If caught in one, stay calm, call for help and swim parallel to shore.

South Beach Park. Our closest beach, with a boardwalk through the dunes, lifeguards, restrooms and free parking. 1704 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Round Island Oceanside Park. Ocean beach plus manatee viewing and kayaking on the river side. Check lifeguard hours. 2201 S Hwy A1A, Vero Beach, FL

Humiston Park. Easiest beach day: lifeguards, restrooms, a kids’ playground, and Ocean Drive shops and restaurants steps away. Busiest, so arrive early. 3000 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Jaycee Beach Park. Family favorite with a long boardwalk, picnic pavilions and a playground. Check lifeguard hours. 4200 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Wabasso Beach Park. Surfing and snorkeling on a near-shore reef. Small lot, so go off-hours. 1808 Wabasso Beach Rd, Vero Beach, FL

Things to do

McKee Botanical Garden. A beautiful local treasure with 18 acres of tropical gardens. 350 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Pelican Island National Wildlife Refuge. Trails and birding at the nation's first wildlife refuge. 4055 Wildlife Way, Vero Beach, FL

Summer Crush Vineyard & Winery. A great day out for live music and drinks, about 30 minutes away. Check their website to see who is playing. 4200 Johnston Rd, Fort Pierce, FL

Environmental Learning Center. Kayak excursions, plus dolphin and bird cruises on the Indian River Lagoon. A great day with school-age kids, and easy to combine with a trip to Sebastian Inlet. 255 Live Oak Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Sebastian Inlet State Park. Fishing, surfing and beaches inside the state park. 9700 S Hwy A1A, Melbourne Beach, FL

McLarty Treasure Museum. Coins, tools and artifacts from the 1715 Spanish treasure fleet, on the site of the survivors' camp, inside Sebastian Inlet State Park. Check hours before you go. 13180 N Hwy A1A, Vero Beach, FL

Vero Beach Museum of Art. A great rainy-day stop, with free admission on the second Saturday of every month. 3001 Riverside Park Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Riverside Theatre. Live shows year-round. 3250 Riverside Park Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Indian River Citrus Museum. Local citrus history in a 1935 landmark. 2140 14th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Hallstrom House. A preserved 1918 pioneer homestead. 1723 Old Dixie Hwy SW, Vero Beach, FL

Historical Society Railroad Station. The restored 1903 depot. 2336 14th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Historic Dodgertown. The Dodgers' spring-training home from 1948. 3901 26th St, Vero Beach, FL

National Navy UDT-SEAL Museum. The only museum dedicated to Navy SEALs and their predecessors, the frogmen who trained on this coast. About 25 minutes south in Fort Pierce. Closed Mondays, so check hours before you go. 3300 N Hwy A1A, Fort Pierce, FL

Vero Bowl Lanes & Lounge. 32 lanes, a full bar and grill, arcade games and pool tables. A good rainy-day or evening option, and cosmic bowling with the lights down is a fun extra. 929 14th Ln, Vero Beach, FL 32960

Majestic 11 Movie Theater. First-run movies at a local theater, right next door to Vero Bowl, so the two make an easy night out. 940 14th Ln, Vero Beach, FL 32960

BigShots Golf Vero Beach. A Topgolf-like experience: climate-controlled hitting bays with fun target games, plus a full restaurant and bars. Great for groups, families and a relaxed evening out. 3456 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL 32960

Ocean Drive shopping. More than 20 local boutiques and galleries near Humiston Park. 3149 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Vero Beach Farmers Market. Saturdays, 8 am to noon, at Humiston Park. 3000 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL
