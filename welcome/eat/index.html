Our local favorites and where to shop.

Eat and drink

Hours and menus change, so check before you go.

Breakfast

Lemon Tree. An easygoing favorite of locals and snowbirds alike. 3125 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Mrs. Mac's Fillin' Station. An old-school diner with gas-station decor and hearty plates. 951 Old Dixie Hwy, Vero Beach, FL

CJ Cannon's. A classic diner at the Vero Beach airport, serving breakfast since 1984. 3414 Cherokee Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Casual

Casey's Place. What we think is the best burger in Vero, with outdoor seating. 917 Azalea Ln, Vero Beach, FL

Vincent's Pizza & Brew. A family-owned locals' favorite since 1978. 510 21st St, Vero Beach, FL

Waldo's at the Driftwood. Fish tacos with an ocean view in a historic 1930s beachcomber inn. 3150 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

El Sid Taqueria. Tacos and empanadas to eat outside or take to the beach. 3300 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Riverside Café. Casual waterfront dining with sunsets over the Indian River. 3341 Bridge Plaza Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Twisted Lime. Tacos, cocktails and Southern and Latin-inspired dishes. 710 S US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Kata Japanese & Thai. Sushi and Thai favorites downtown. 1306 20th St, Vero Beach, FL

Chill and Grill. Good food and a locals' favorite, handy on the way north to Sebastian. 7401 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL 32967

Hogan Yards. House-brewed beer, Central Texas BBQ smoked daily and social courts day and night. Check their website for events during your stay. 2745 St Lucie Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Italian Grill. Family-run Southern Italian with generous portions on the west side of town, serving Vero since 1993. 2180 58th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Good seafood

12A Buoy. Hands down the best seafood around, about 25 minutes south and well worth the drive. The vibe is very casual. There are no reservations, so it is ideal to go during non-peak hours, like 3 or 4 pm, to avoid a very long wait. 22 Fishermans Wharf, Fort Pierce, FL

Green Marlin Restaurant & Raw Bar. A locals' favorite for relaxed seafood, a raw bar and a full bar. 1475 US Highway 1, Vero Beach, FL

Tradewinds 41. Steak and seafood with a two-story tiki bar overlooking the Indian River. 41 Royal Palm Pointe, Vero Beach, FL

Date night

The Tides. A snowbird favorite for seafood fine dining. Reservations only, (772) 234-3966. 3103 Cardinal Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Havana Nights Piano Bar. Live music in a Caribbean-inspired piano bar above Maison Martinique. 1605 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Ocean Grill. Oceanfront dining since 1965. 1050 Beachland Blvd, Vero Beach, FL

Maison Martinique. Romantic French-Mediterranean. Reservations recommended. 1605 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Curfew. Creative contemporary American. 1932 14th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Vero Prime. A polished steakhouse. 2023 14th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Post & Vine. Thoughtful dishes and a curated wine list. 1919 14th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Coffee, treats and healthy

Coffee House 1420. Locally roasted coffee. 2001 14th Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Cravings. Pastries and homemade ice cream on a cozy patio. 3149 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Countryside Citrus. Our beach-day tip: an orange creamsicle soft serve, then tacos next door at El Sid. 3300 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Drinks, music and breweries

Grind + Grape. Espresso by day, wine and live music most nights. 925 Bougainvillea Ln, Vero Beach, FL

Sol Mar Cocktail Lounge. Cocktails on Ocean Drive. 2855 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Walking Tree Brewery. Family-friendly, in a 1945 Navy warehouse, with food trucks. 3209 Dodger Rd, Vero Beach, FL

American Icon Brewery. Craft beer and a full menu downtown. 1133 19th Pl, Vero Beach, FL

Orchid Island Brewery. Vero's first homegrown brewery, now inside Hogan Yards, with beers brewed using local Indian River citrus. 2745 St Lucie Ave, Vero Beach, FL

Costa d'Este Beach Resort. Oceanfront drinks with a view. 3244 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Kimpton Vero Beach Hotel & Spa. Oceanfront cocktails and dining at Cobalt. 3500 Ocean Dr, Vero Beach, FL

Filthy's Fine Cocktails & Beer. Live bands, DJs and Thursday karaoke. 1238 16th St, Vero Beach, FL

Kilted Mermaid. Wine, craft beer, small plates and live music. 1937 Old Dixie Hwy, Vero Beach, FL

Sebastian favorites (about 25 to 45 minutes north)

Captain Hiram's Resort. A laid-back riverfront spot with a Bahamian-style sand bar, live music most nights and boat rentals on the Indian River. 1580 US Highway 1, Sebastian, FL

Yellow Dog Cafe. Comfort food with a flair and beautiful Indian River views. Reservations recommended. Just north of Sebastian. 905 US Highway 1, Malabar, FL
