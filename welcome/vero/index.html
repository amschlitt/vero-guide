About Vero Beach

Vero Beach is a relaxed, low-rise town on the Treasure Coast. It takes its name from a Spanish treasure fleet wrecked offshore by a 1715 hurricane, and coins still wash up today. Building rules here favor “sunrises, not high-rises,” which keeps the shoreline uncrowded.
